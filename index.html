Yuang Zhang

I am a researcher at Tencent. My research focuses on video generation, with broad interests spanning autonomous systems and computer vision.

I have published at top-tier venues including Nature Machine Intelligence, ICML, CVPR, ECCV, ICCV. My representative works include MimicMotion for controllable human motion video generation (ICML 2025, ⭐2.5k on GitHub) and the MOTR series for end-to-end multi-object tracking (ECCV 2022 & CVPR 2023).
